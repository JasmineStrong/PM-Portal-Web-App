inHealth Precision Medicine

inHealth Precision Medicine at Johns Hopkins is whole-person medicine: constructing the understanding of patient subgroups at the most granular level, drawing from multiple insights and disease-specific expertise. As one of the country’s earliest learning health systems, inHealth Precision Medicine is part of the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine's deep commitment to help physicians and clinician scientists learn from their patients, study diseases, and better characterize sub-groups of populations. The result is the opportunity to develop more precise care for every patient.

Integrating Health Data to Enable Better Care

The Precision Medicine Analytics Platform (PMAP), a cloud-based tool offered by inHealth, is an engine to accelerate biomedical research that provides opportunities to develop more precise care for every patient.
