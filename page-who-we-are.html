Who We Are

inHealth Precision Medicine Leadership

inHealth Precision Medicine is our strategic initiative to advance precision medicine at Johns Hopkins.  Our dedicated, multi-disciplinary team guides strategy, provides best practices, and advises on the most current data collection and analysis processes, including secure storage and sharing across stakeholder groups. We also provide critical insights on the design and development of clinical and research practice groups that help to move data from discovery to delivery - turning insights into improvements in patient care. 

Recognized as pioneers in precision medicine technologies, we continue to scale across the Johns Hopkins Medicine enterprise, serving as a model for health care institutions globally.
